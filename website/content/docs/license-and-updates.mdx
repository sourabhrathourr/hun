Hun is a one-time purchase of $9. One license key works on up to 3 Macs, and updates are included.

## Activate

The first time you open Hun, it asks for a key on **Activate Hun**:

1. Paste the key from your purchase email into **License key**.
2. Click **Activate Hun**.

Hun stores the key in your Keychain. If you don't have a key yet, click **Buy Hun · $9**.

Until Hun is activated, the menu bar panel says **Hun needs a license**, with **Open Hun** to get to the activation screen.

## Move Hun to another Mac

A key that's already on 3 Macs can't be activated on a fourth. To move it:

1. On a Mac you no longer use, open **Settings › License**.
2. Click **Deactivate…**, then **Deactivate**. Hun asks for the key again before it can be used on that Mac.
3. Activate on the new Mac.

If you try a key that's on as many Macs as it allows, Hun says: "This key is already active on as many Macs as it allows. Deactivate Hun on one of them and try again."

## Offline use

Hun checks your license with the license server when it opens, every 6 hours, and when your Mac wakes. If it can't reach the server, Hun keeps working for 72 hours after the last successful check. **Settings › License** shows **Offline grace period** while that's the case, and **Last verified** says when the last check succeeded.

After 72 hours without a successful check, Hun shows **Could not verify your license**. Connect to the internet and click **Try again**.

<Callout>
If the server says the key is no longer valid, Hun asks for a key again, with the message "This license is no longer valid."
</Callout>

## Updates

Hun checks for updates in the background. When a new version is ready, a line appears at the bottom of the sidebar, such as **Hun 0.4.4 is ready**.

- Click the version to see what's new in the changelog.
- Click the download arrow next to it to install the update.

To check yourself, choose **Check for Updates…** from the app menu.

Updates are signed and installed with Sparkle. The `hun` command on your PATH points into the app, so it updates with it.
